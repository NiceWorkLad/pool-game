How XP works
  • Learn: 25 XP for each new drill, 75 more for finishing a lesson.
  • Puzzles: 50 XP × the difficulty the first time you solve one, 10 XP after that.
  • Survival: 10 XP + 10 × the difficulty for every position cleared, 50 XP for a new best run.
  • Blackout: 100 XP for clearing the table, plus 5 XP for every shot under 50, and 50 XP for a new best.
  • Two players: 40 XP for every frame you finish.
  • Each level needs 50 XP more than the one before.
